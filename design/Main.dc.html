How does hill descent control work?

Hill Descent Control (HDC) actively brakes the vehicle on a descent so you can come down a steep slope at a constant, safe speed.

  • Rated for a maximum gradient of 50%.
  • Off by default, so press the HDC switch after each restart to enable it.

DRIVING OPERATION

Automatic release

With the AVH on and the gearshift lever in the D position, step on the accelerator pedal, then AVH will be automatically released, and the parking brake will be automatically released.

HDC

The HDC helps the driver actively brake the vehicle in downhill conditions (the maximum gradient is 50%) to ensure that the driver can drive down steep slopes at a constant and safety speed.
